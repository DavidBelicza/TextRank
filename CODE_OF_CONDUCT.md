# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project is welcome, regardless of experience,
background or identity. We want reporting a bug, asking a question or sending a
pull request to be a good experience for everyone involved.

## Expected Behaviour

* Be respectful and assume good intent.
* Give and accept constructive feedback about the code, not the person.
* Keep discussions on topic and useful to others reading later.
* Respect that the maintainers decide what fits the goals of this project.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

* Personal attacks, insults or harassment of any kind.
* Discriminatory or sexual language and imagery.
* Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
* Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring maintainer decisions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space where someone represents this project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer at david@belicza.com. Reports
are handled privately. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close
issues and pull requests, or block accounts that break these rules.
